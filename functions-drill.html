Entire and functional

A relation f ⊆ A × B is entire if every element of A sends out at least one arrow, and functional if every element of A sends out at most one. A function is both: exactly one arrow out of each element of A. The elements of B can receive any number of arrows, including none and including several. Classify relations given by arrow diagrams, lists of pairs and formulas, find the element that breaks the rule, and decide when two functions are equal. A miss pauses the clock, marks the culprit in red, and explains itself.
