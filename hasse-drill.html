Hasse diagrams

In a Hasse diagram, y sits above x and is joined to it exactly when y covers x: x ≺ y with nothing strictly between. Then x ⪯ y exactly when you can climb from x to y. Read orders off diagrams, find maximal and greatest elements and least upper bounds, spot covers, and catch drawings that are not Hasse diagrams. A miss pauses the clock and explains itself.
