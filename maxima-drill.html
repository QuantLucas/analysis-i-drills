Maxima and bounds

A number M is a maximum of f on A when (i) f(x) ≤ M for every x ∈ A and (ii) f(y) = M for some y ∈ A. Find maxima on finite sets and intervals, spot which condition fails, and practise the moves of the proof. A miss pauses the clock and explains itself. Keys 1 to 4 pick an answer.
